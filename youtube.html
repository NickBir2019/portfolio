            What I found

            1. The channel grows with game launches, then fades fast. 2019, when Black Desert came to Xbox and PS4, brought 56% of all-time views. The best month ever was November 2024, with 218,426 views, a month after Throne and Liberty launched. Four months later it was down to 6,460. Between launches, views dropped to around 340 a month in 2023.
            2. People find the channel through search, and guides are what they're searching for. YouTube search brought 43% of views. Guide and build videos got a median of 7,420 views, against 4,322 for other videos. They're a quarter of the edited videos but half of the views. The top video, a "should you play?" guide before Black Desert's PS4 launch, brought 14% of the channel's views on its own.
            3. Edited videos do far better than streams and Shorts. Edited videos got a median of 5,004 views and 4.6 new subscribers per 1,000 views. Live streams got 1,043 views and 2.2 subscribers per 1,000. Streams were a fifth of all uploads but brought under 5% of views.
            4. 5 to 15 minutes works best. Videos of this length got medians of around 6,000 to 6,500 views. Videos under 5 minutes got 2,513, and videos over 20 minutes got 1,141.

            Upload day made no clear difference. One caution: these are lifetime totals, so older videos have had longer to build up views.

            How I used AI

            I used Claude, an AI assistant, for the data work and to build this page. I set the questions and made the calls. The AI did the heavy lifting, and I checked what it produced.

            What I asked forWhat happened, and how it was checked
            01Unzip the three YouTube Studio exports and describe every fileIt found that the daily "Table data" file stops at the top 500 days. The dashboard uses the full daily totals file instead.
            02Check the three exports agreeTotal views match across all three: 1,799,849. The video list adds up to 3,309 fewer, which is views on videos no longer on the channel.
            03Group videos by game and typeGames are tagged from keywords in the title. The first pass left 30 videos untagged, and after reviewing those titles 8 remain, mostly test streams and team matches. Anything 40 minutes or longer counts as a stream, 60 seconds or under as a Short.
            04Flag anything odd in the data35 videos show negative subscribers (people unsubscribing after watching), 20 have no publish date and 4 have no views. These were left in and are shown as they are.
            05Compare groups fairlyMedians rather than averages, because one video has 14% of all views and would skew any average.
            06Build a dashboard that reruns from new exportsEvery figure is calculated in the page from the raw CSV files, and the finding numbers were cross-checked against a separate analysis.

            Why this matters at work

            The same approach works for any operational report: pull the raw export, check it adds up, agree the rules for grouping, and build something that reruns each month. AI speeds up the build, but the questions and the checking still need a person who knows the operation.
