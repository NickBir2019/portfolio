About

I'm a Customer Resolutions Advisor on the technical team at British Gas. I take over complaints once they're logged and keep ownership through to resolution, usually with 40 to 50 open at a time, including cases where other teams need to act.

On secondment as a Performance Analyst I tracked complaints and call centre KPIs in Power BI and Excel and presented findings to senior managers. I also created a training pack on first point of contact resolution. I've supported 8 new complaint handlers through a 12-week training programme and completed the British Gas Aspire to Manager Programme.

I'm working towards team leadership in complaints and customer operations.
