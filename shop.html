When I co-founded an online supplements business, we tracked sales, stock, website traffic and ad campaigns through the e-commerce store's own reports. This page brings that kind of reporting together in one dashboard. It uses made-up data, but the situations it shows are real ones we dealt with.

      How it was done then

      Before AI tools, each part of this lived in a different place. Sales and stock came from the e-commerce store's own reports, and website traffic and ad results came from Google and Facebook's own dashboards.

      Nick to write, in his own words, the real story behind situations 1 and 2: roughly when it happened, what was decided and why, and what the outcome was. Keep it to what he can talk about in an interview. No real figures unless he's happy to share them.

      How I built this

      I described the real situations to Claude, an AI assistant, which generated a year of made-up data that behaves the same way. It then built this page to calculate every figure from that data. The data links together throughout: visits turn into orders, orders use up stock, and ad spend brings in visits.

      Having it all in one place is the point. Now a stock problem, a traffic drop and the ad response can be seen side by side, instead of across three separate reports.
