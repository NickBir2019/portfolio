Automating complaint response letters

Every complaint ends with a final response letter. Writing one by hand meant copying details between systems and rewriting information that already sat in the complaint file. I was part of the team that mapped the process, suggested where AI could help, wrote the prompt behind it and tested the tool that was built.

The problem

A final response letter has to include the full detail of the complaint, the investigation that was carried out, the outcome, and the regulatory information the customer is entitled to. All of this was put together by hand.

Handlers also had to copy the customer's name, address and account number one field at a time from the account system into the letter. Every copy and paste was a chance to get something wrong, and the details are going to a customer in a regulated letter.

What I did

  1. Mapped the processWith my team, I explained the process from start to finish to the external company building the tool, using a process map.
  2. Suggested ideasWe pointed out where the manual work was and suggested what could be automated, including pulling customer details in so handlers no longer copied them across.
  3. Wrote the promptI was asked to write the prompt that gets AI to produce the letter. Most of the work was refining it again and again until the letters were right.
  4. Handed it overI shared the finished prompt with the automation team, who built it into the tool.
  5. Tested itMy team tested the tool and suggested improvements.

Result

The AI-produced letter is now mandatory for every complaint handler, and feedback from handlers has been positive. Customer details are no longer copied across by hand, which removes a common source of errors.

There are no figures yet on time saved, so I haven't claimed any.
